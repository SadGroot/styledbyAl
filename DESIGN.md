## Overview

Styled by Al is an English-language portfolio for a Kerala-based celebrity, bridal, and personal stylist. Its agreed direction is dark couture: photography leads, with near-black surfaces, warm ivory typography, and a restrained antique-gold accent.

## Typography

Use an expressive editorial serif for display headings and a clean sans serif for navigation, forms, and supporting copy. Keep captions separate from the photographic compositions.

## Layout

Balance bridal and celebrity/editorial work on the homepage. Progress from a typographic introduction into selected work, services, an About preview, and an enquiry invitation. Use generous space and asymmetric photographic compositions. Keep navigation and contact actions available independently of the exhibition.

## Elevation & Depth

Use a real WebGL camera journey through floating photographic panels. Adapt texture count and effects to phone capabilities. Preserve a standard editorial gallery for reduced motion, unavailable WebGL, or poor performance.

## Components

Keep exhibition captions, project links, and navigation in accessible HTML. Services explain their process and lead to a preselected bespoke enquiry. The owner edits through a private, mobile-friendly content studio.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Identify preview photography clearly until genuine work is supplied.
- Preserve the ability to browse work and enquire without WebGL.
- Keep pricing private.
- Keep email and WhatsApp inactive until genuine contact details are configured.
